St. Aveline
Opening scene

Static image of a forest from ground level. Trees look beautiful. Sun is shining through the tree tops. Birds chirping. Animal noises in the distance..

Two young kids quickly run past the camera. Laughing and happy.

“St. Aveline 1955” Wipes on screen as the kids run past.

Camera follows the kids as they chase each other through the forest.

Narrator: "Do you remember when you lost your innocence?”

Soldiers shouting in the distance

Narrator: “I do.”

Children hide behind rocks

Voyeuristic camera view of villagers lined up infront of a ditch.

Zoom out to show soldiers pointing gun at villagers.

Narrator: “Some things shouldn’t be seen, especially by children.”

Zoom in on the young child’s eye. Focus on the reflection of her eye of the soldiers and villagers.

Commander: Ready… Aim… Fire…

Show the villagers be gunned down in the reflection of her eye.

She blinks.

Cut to black

Music starts

Fade in to a camera flying over a lush green forest.

Camera flies over an army checkpoint. An army soldier is rummaging through a car and another soldier pushes an old lady on the ground..

Camera flies around the town square where the military is matching.

Camera flies to the castle there is a general smoking a cigar on a balcony.


“St. Aveline” Title displays on screen.

Music stops

Cut to black

Fade in.

Focus on female main character closed eye as a young adult.

She slowly opens her eye.

Zoom out from her eye showing a reflection of a family in an apartment.

Main Character: “Be careful papa. There are a lot of guards out tonight.”

Papa: “Don’t worry my darling. I’m always careful. I’ll be back soon”

Papa leaves the apartment, but he forgot his papers. He’ll surely be grabbed by the guards without his papers.

Enter gameplay as lead character..
